## What does a real appliance leak cleanup response actually include?

A real response starts with extraction, not a mop and towels. A crew pulls standing water with a truck-mounted or portable extractor, then uses a moisture meter to map exactly how far the water traveled, including under cabinets, behind toe kicks, and inside wall cavities you can't see.

Washing machine and dishwasher leaks almost always run under the appliance and into the subfloor before anyone notices. That's why moisture mapping matters more than a visual check. A technician will probe the toe kick under cabinets, lift baseboards if drywall is wicking water, and set air movers and a low-grain-refrigerant dehumidifier based on what the readings show, not a guess. This is the drying approach laid out in the [IICRC S500 standard](https://iicrc.org/s500/), the industry reference for water damage restoration. In Honolulu's humidity, skipping this step is how a one-day leak turns into a mold problem inside 48 hours.

If the water sat for more than a day, you're also looking at antimicrobial treatment on affected framing, not just drying. A company that only talks about "drying out" the floor without mentioning moisture readings or a drying log is skipping steps.

## Washing machine, dishwasher, fridge, water heater, or AC condensate: who fixes what?

The appliance itself is a plumber's or appliance tech's job; the water damage left behind is a restoration company's job, and you may need both on the same call. A washing machine supply hose, dishwasher line, or refrigerator ice-maker line that bursts is usually a quick valve shutoff followed by a hose or line replacement. A water heater tank failure is bigger: once the tank itself fails, it typically needs full replacement, and the water volume involved is much higher.

AC condensate line clogs are a slower, quieter version of the same problem; water backs up and drips for days before anyone notices a stain on the ceiling or a damp closet. By the time it's visible, the saturation is often worse than it looks, which is why water leak detection matters for intermittent or hidden leaks, not just obvious bursts.

A restoration company's job in all of these cases is the same regardless of which appliance failed: extract the water, dry the structure, and document it. The [emergency water removal](/services/emergency-water-removal/) team handles that part; a plumber or appliance repair tech handles the mechanical fix.

## Do you need a plumber and a restoration company, or just one?

You need both when the leak is still active or the source hasn't been fixed yet, and you need the restoration side alone once the source is stopped and only drying remains. If water is actively running, the first call is whoever can shut it off, either you at the supply valve or a plumber if the shutoff itself is failing.

Once the source is stopped, the clock starts on drying. Waiting a day "to see if it dries on its own" in Honolulu's trade-wind humidity is the single most common mistake homeowners make, because visible mold can start forming in the same window. A water heater flood cleanup after a tank failure, for example, usually means a plumber installs the new unit while the restoration crew runs extraction and drying in parallel, not one after the other.

## What should you check before hiring an appliance leak cleanup company in Honolulu?

Ask whether they document moisture readings in writing, because that documentation is what your insurance adjuster will actually look at. A company that hands you a drying log with daily readings gives your claim a paper trail; one that just says "it's dry now" gives you nothing to show an adjuster.

Few other things worth confirming on the phone before you commit:

- **Availability.** AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care runs a 24/7 emergency line, which matters because appliance leaks don't wait for business hours.
- **Condo and high-rise experience.** Waikiki, Kakaako, and Ala Moana towers add HOA and AOAO insurance coordination most single-family jobs don't require. Ask if they've handled a unit-to-unit water claim before.
- **Equipment on the truck.** LGR dehumidifiers and air movers should already be in the vehicle, not ordered after the estimate.
- **Written scope before work starts.** Every loss is different, and a company that gives you a written scope before starting work is one that isn't guessing at the price later.

If an appliance failure is sudden and accidental, most homeowners policies cover the resulting water damage, though coverage details vary by policy; see the [Insurance Information Institute's overview of standard homeowners coverage](https://www.iii.org/article/what-is-covered-by-standard-homeowners-insurance) for the general pattern. Gradual leaks from long-term wear are treated differently by most insurers, so documentation of when the failure happened matters.

## What happens when the leak reaches the unit below in a Kakaako or Waikiki high-rise?

A leak that travels to the unit below becomes two claims instead of one, and usually involves both the unit owner's policy and the building's AOAO master policy. The restoration crew needs to document moisture readings in both units separately, because the adjuster handling the downstairs ceiling damage is often not the same insurer handling your unit.
